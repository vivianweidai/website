# archives/ — reference materials (private, never served)

These files live at the repo **top level, outside `public/`**, so they are never published to vivianweidai.com. Filenames and contents disclose UNR/UBC faculty, lab, and location references — keep it that way.

This is a **legend**, not a data source. Nothing here is built or fetched by the site; read it to understand what each artifact is and whether it still maps to the live Research page.

## Page vs. archive: they diverge on purpose

On **2026-07-01** the Research page was culled to only techs/toys we can **actively engage with** — Access-principle Tiers 1–3 (home lab · UNR SIL walk-in · remote observatories), not "everything that exists at the university" (commit `0fe0712` and its neighbors). The archive still holds the **full scoping survey**, so it lists more instruments and more of the university landscape than the page shows. That gap is intentional: the archive is the survey and the decision record; the page is the curated live subset. Don't re-add archive-only instruments to the page just because a folder exists here.
