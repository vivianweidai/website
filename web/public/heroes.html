Hero candidates

One tile per project on the wall, so each hero has to say at a glance which of the three it is. Landscape and square tiles span two columns of the wall, portraits span one — so the shape matters as much as the picture. The tag on each caption says which.

Astrometry — position and motion

Measuring where things are, and catching them move. The distinguishing look is a moving object against fixed stars.

Photometry — brightness over time

Measuring how bright something is, repeatedly, until a shape appears. The distinguishing look is a curve, or the same star visibly dimmer.

Spectroscopy — light split by wavelength

The distinguishing look is a rainbow, or a trace with gaps in it.
